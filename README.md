**How the OKX Card Works**

The card lives inside the OKX app under the "Pay" tab. Your OKX Pay wallet holds stablecoins — fund it via ACH, bank wire, or by depositing crypto from another wallet and converting in-app. When you tap to pay anywhere, OKX converts the stablecoin balance to the merchant's local currency on the spot, using the Mastercard or Visa network for settlement.

A few things worth knowing about the mechanics:

- Your crypto stays in your wallet until the exact moment of purchase. There's no pre-loading or manual top-up step.
- Supported stablecoins vary slightly by region: **USDC and USDG** for EEA and US users; **USDT, USDC, and USDG** for South Asia and Africa.
- Cashback applies **only to USDG-funded purchases**. Pay with USDC or USDT and you earn nothing back. This is the single most important detail to set up correctly — go to card management, set "Spending order" to USDG first.

The card provisions directly into Apple Pay and Google Pay. For online purchases, you get a card number, expiry date, and CVV inside the app.

**What the Card Is Good For (and What It Isn't)**

The OKX Card makes real sense in a few specific situations:

- **You already hold stablecoins on OKX.** The card costs nothing to issue, there's no monthly fee, and your USDG earns 3.5%+ just sitting in the wallet. Adding the card is essentially free.
- **You travel internationally.** Zero FX fee plus a tiny conversion spread is among the most competitive setups in crypto cards right now.
- **You're trading actively and already at VIP tier.** At VIP 1+, the cashback math becomes more meaningful — $100–$1,500/month in USDG is worth the effort.

Where it falls short:
- **Regular users who want real rewards on everyday spending.** The $5 monthly cap means you max out at $250 of effective cashback spend. You'd need to cross into VIP 1 ($100K in assets) before the card becomes a serious rewards tool.
- **Users in excluded US states** (Hawaii, Kentucky, New York, Nevada, Texas, West Virginia). No workaround.
- **Anyone who needs ATM cash.** Not supported in most regions as of mid-2026.
- **Users primarily on Coinbase or Kraken.** Moving balances to OKX for $5 of monthly cashback doesn't make financial sense unless you value OKX's trading platform for other reasons.
